## About Me

I'm a Software Engineer focused on **Backend Engineering and Blockchain & Web3**, with a strong interest in system design, software architecture, and the engineering principles behind reliable software.

My approach to software engineering goes beyond making code work. I care about understanding how systems operate internally, designing maintainable architectures, solving complex problems, and building reliable software with performance, scalability, and long term maintainability in mind.

I value strong fundamentals over dependence on a particular language or framework. My interests span backend systems, computer science fundamentals, and decentralized applications, with an emphasis on understanding why engineering decisions matter and how they affect the final product.
